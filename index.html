the building chapter

Then I started building things.

Some were scientific. Some were data-heavy. All of them started with a question.

01 · Genomics Copilot

VariantLens

Built an AI-assisted genomic research platform that brings together 3,447 variant–trait records across 19 trait groups for natural-language exploration, comparison and evidence tracing. Combined structured genomic data with an LLM layer to generate evidence-grounded insights while using citations and guardrails to distinguish statistical associations from unsupported causal claims.

02 · AI

LLM Evaluation

Evaluated an LLM chatbot across contested science, high-stakes health claims and informal/code-mixed inputs, then translated failure patterns into product-facing recommendations. Scored responses against trusted sources, identified inconsistent confidence calibration and recurring format-compliance failures

pythonllm evaluationqa
03 · Bioinformatics × Product Thinking

Genomic Variant–Phenotype Database

At GeneArche Wellness, I worked on turning fragmented genomic information into a structured, queryable health-data resource. I consolidated and cross-validated GWAS and variant–trait evidence across 19 health-related traits, designed the database structure and data workflow. The resource was later scaled to incorporate more traits.

The curated knowledge base was also meant to support GeneArche's conversational AI work, providing structured health and genomic information as a foundation for training its conversational health assistant.
